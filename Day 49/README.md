📚 Revision Day 35–49 — From RNNs to Transformers 🧠

Continuing my 100 Days of Deep Learning journey, I revised the concepts from Day 35 to Day 49, covering the evolution of sequence models from RNNs → LSTMs → GRUs → Seq2Seq → Attention → Transformers.

🔹 What I Revised
RNN (Recurrent Neural Network) — hidden states, sequential processing, and shared parameters.
LSTM — forget, input, and output gates for capturing long-term dependencies.
GRU — simplified RNN architecture using update and reset gates.
Bidirectional RNN — learning information from both past and future context.
Seq2Seq Architecture — encoder-decoder framework for sequence-to-sequence tasks.
Attention Mechanism — assigning different importance to input tokens.
Bahdanau & Luong Attention — additive vs. multiplicative attention.
Beam Search — maintaining multiple candidate sequences during decoding.
Transformer Architecture — replacing recurrence with attention and enabling parallel processing.
Self-Attention — using Query, Key, and Value to understand relationships between tokens.
Positional Encoding — adding positional information to sequences.
Transformer Summary — Encoder self-attention + feed-forward and Decoder self-attention + cross-attention.
💡 Key Takeaway

This revision helped me connect the evolution of sequence modelling:

RNN → LSTM/GRU → Seq2Seq → Attention → Self-Attention → Transformer

Instead of just memorizing individual architectures, I’m focusing on understanding why each development was needed and what limitation it addressed.
