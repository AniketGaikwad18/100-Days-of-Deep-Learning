Continuing my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey with Day 22.

I covered:

The role of optimizers in model training
Challenges like learning rate, local minima, and saddle points
Exponentially Weighted Moving Average (EWMA)
How β controls the balance between recent and previous values
Why EWMA is useful for smoothing and reducing noise
Its connection to optimizers such as Adam and RMSProp

The focus was on understanding the intuition and mathematical formulation behind optimization rather than simply using optimizers as a library function.
