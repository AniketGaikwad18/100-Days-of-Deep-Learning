Day 25 — Adam Optimizer ⚙️

Day 25 of my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey.

Today I learned about Adam (Adaptive Moment Estimation), an optimizer that combines the ideas of Momentum and RMSProp to adapt the learning rate for each parameter.

I covered:

First & second moment estimates
Bias correction
Adaptive learning rates
Advantages and limitations of Adam
Variants like AdamW, Nadam, and AMSGrad

Another step toward understanding what actually happens behind the optimization process of neural networks.
