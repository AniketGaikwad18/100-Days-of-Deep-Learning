Day 46 — Introduction to Transformers 🤖

Day 46 of my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey.

Today I started learning the basics of the Transformer architecture, a major step forward from the RNN/Seq2Seq concepts I studied earlier.

What I learned:
What a Transformer is and why it is used for sequence data
How Transformers use attention instead of recurrence
Basic Encoder–Decoder architecture
Self-Attention in the encoder and decoder
Cross-Attention between encoder and decoder
How the encoder processes the input sequence and produces contextual representations
How the decoder generates the output sequence
Why Transformers are highly parallelizable during training
How they help capture long-range dependencies

The original Transformer architecture was introduced in Attention Is All You Need, which proposed an architecture based entirely on attention mechanisms rather than recurrence or convolution.
