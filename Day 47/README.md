Day 47 — Understanding Transformers Beyond the Basics 🤖🧠

Day 47 of my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey.

Today, I went beyond the basic Transformer architecture and explored a research survey, “A Comprehensive Survey on Applications of Transformers for Deep Learning Tasks.”

What I learned:
Self-Attention and Scaled Dot-Product Attention
The role of Query (Q), Key (K), and Value (V)
Multi-Head Attention and why multiple attention heads are useful
Transformer Encoder and Decoder architecture
Masked Multi-Head Attention in the decoder
Residual Connections, Add & Norm, and Feed-Forward layers
How Transformers capture long-range dependencies and enable parallel processing
Transformer applications beyond NLP, including Computer Vision, Multi-Modality, Audio & Speech, and Signal Processing

One important takeaway was realizing that Transformers are not limited to language tasks. Their attention-based architecture has been explored across multiple domains and deep learning tasks.
