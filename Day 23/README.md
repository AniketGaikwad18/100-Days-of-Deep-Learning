Day 23 — SGD with Momentum 🧠

Continuing my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey with Day 23.

Today I learned about SGD with Momentum, an optimization technique that improves standard SGD by using information from previous updates.

I focused on:

Why standard SGD can oscillate during optimization
How momentum accumulates past gradients to build velocity
The mathematical formulation of the update rule
The role of β (momentum coefficient)
How momentum helps achieve faster and more stable convergence
Its behavior around local minima, saddle points, and global minima
The possibility of overshooting when momentum is too high

The key idea I understood today: instead of making every update independently, momentum uses the direction of previous updates to move more smoothly toward the minimum.
