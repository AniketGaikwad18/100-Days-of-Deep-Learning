Day 17 — Dropout Regularization 🧠

Continuing my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey with Day 17.

Today I learned about Dropout, a regularization technique used to reduce overfitting in neural networks.

During training, dropout randomly deactivates a certain percentage of neurons so the network doesn't become overly dependent on specific neurons.

I also studied:

Dropout rate and its effect on overfitting/underfitting
Its use in ANN, CNN, and RNN
How dropout can affect convergence and loss

The key takeaway: Dropout encourages the network to learn more robust features instead of memorizing the training data.
