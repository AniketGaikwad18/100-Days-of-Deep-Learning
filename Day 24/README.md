Day 24 — NAG (Nesterov Accelerated Gradient) 🚀

Continuing my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey with Day 24.

Today I learned about Nesterov Accelerated Gradient (NAG), an optimization technique that improves upon classical momentum by looking ahead before computing the gradient.

I explored:

The look-ahead position and why it is used
Mathematical formulation of NAG
How NAG differs from SGD with Momentum
How it helps reduce oscillations and overshooting
Why it can lead to faster and more stable convergence

The key idea: Momentum asks “where am I going?” while NAG first looks ahead and then decides how to correct the direction.
