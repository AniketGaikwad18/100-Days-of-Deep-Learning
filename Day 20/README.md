Day 20 — Weight Initialization in Neural Networks 🧠

Continuing my #100DaysOfDeepLearning journey with Day 20.

Today I learned about weight initialization techniques and why poor initialization can affect neural network training.

I explored:

Zero initialization and why it causes neurons to behave similarly.
Non-zero constant initialization and the problem of neurons still acting alike.
Random initialization and its challenges.
How small random values can contribute to the vanishing gradient problem.
How large random values can cause saturation with Sigmoid/Tanh and unstable values with ReLU.

Another step toward understanding what happens inside a neural network during training, rather than just implementing it with code.
