🧠 Day 39 — Deep RNNs (Stacked RNNs)

Continuing my 100 Days of Deep Learning journey with Deep RNNs (Stacked RNNs).

Today, I learned how multiple RNN layers can be stacked on top of each other to increase the depth of the network and learn more hierarchical representations from sequential data.

📚 What I learned:
🔹 Concept of Deep/Stacked RNNs
🔹 How RNN layers are organized across time and depth
🔹 Hidden states at different layers and time steps
🔹 Understanding the role of weight matrices and bias vectors
🔹 RNN cell representation and forward computation
🔹 How deeper RNNs can learn hierarchical representations
🔹 When to consider Deep RNNs — complex tasks, larger datasets, and sufficient computational resources

The key idea I took away today:

An RNN can learn across time, while stacking RNN layers allows the network to learn across depth as well.

I’m continuing to follow my approach of understanding the concept → writing it down → breaking down the architecture → implementing it, so the fundamentals remain strong before moving toward more advanced sequence models.
